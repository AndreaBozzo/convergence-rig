## Limitations

- Tested on a small `30`-row (`28` post-mutation) fixture with a single live equality-delete file; large-scale Spark vs DuckDB performance and multi-file delete scaling are not benchmarked here.
- Spark exports `updated_at` via `unix_micros(updated_at)` under `spark.sql.session.timeZone=UTC` to prevent local Python timezone conversion drift while preserving full microsecond precision; timestamp-type semantics (`timestamp` vs `timestamp_ntz`) are verified separately from `df.schema.jsonValue()`.
- `read_snapshot.py`'s `iceberg_scan_details.scan_nodes` list was empty because Spark's Adaptive Query Execution wrapped `BatchScanExec` inside a `ShuffleQueryStageExec` before `toLocalIterator()`; `EXPLAIN FORMATTED`, `EXPLAIN EXTENDED`, `DESCRIBE EXTENDED`, `.files` and `.manifests` captured the full scan and delete-file evidence.
