# Logo prompt

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Two candidates were generated; the second was selected for its slightly tighter, more balanced silhouette. Both use two angular paths merging into a single aligned output.

Final asset: `logo.png`, 512 by 512 pixels, with transparency preserved. Resized offline with System.Drawing and visually checked at 512 and 64 pixels against white and `#0d1117`. PNG is the fallback because the installed FFmpeg WebP encoder could not be accessed in the sandbox; no encoder was downloaded.

## Initial generation

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: square repository logo for convergence-rig, an open-source local CDC verification lab.
Primary request: create one precise, restrained geometric mark showing two independent paths converging into one exactly aligned output.
Subject: two broad angular tracks enter from the left at different heights, take clean opposing 45-degree bends, and meet in one common horizontal terminal on the right. Make a compact, balanced, unmistakable single symbol. Use solid teal for the upper track and shared terminal, solid ochre for the lower track; crisp junction without overlap effects.
Style: flat vector-like geometry rendered as a raster image, engineering schematic simplicity, squared ends, uniform thick tracks, generous negative space. Dry and functional.
Color palette: only medium teal (#168B88) and ochre (#C88131). Fully transparent background. Both colors must remain legible against white and near-black themes.
Composition: square canvas, centered mark occupying approximately 75 percent of the width and height. Broad shapes readable at 64 by 64 pixels, clean enough for a 512 by 512 final asset.
Constraints: one mark only; no text, letters, words, numbers, borders, shadows, gradients, textures, tiny details, 3D, mockup presentation, or watermark. No elephants, icebergs, snowflakes, database cylinders, mascots, infinity loops, or third-party logos. Real transparency, not a checkerboard illustration.
```

## Refinement used for the selected candidate

The first generated image was supplied as the edit target.

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Edit the previous generated convergence-rig logo as a second candidate.
Preserve its exact two-path merge geometry, broad squared tracks, two-color identity, and fully transparent background.
Refine only its finish and placement: use absolutely uniform solid teal (#168B88) and uniform solid ochre (#C88131), with no tonal variation, texture, gradients, outlines, dark keylines, highlights or shadows. Make the union look precisely cut and fitted rather than layered. Center the complete silhouette optically in the square with equal left/right margins and equal top/bottom margins. Keep broad lines legible at 64 px. No text, letters, decorative details or extra symbols. One symbol only.
```
